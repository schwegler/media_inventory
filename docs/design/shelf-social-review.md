# Selectable shelf Story designs

All three reviewed directions are now selectable in the real shelf renderer, alongside Telly and Facet palettes, optional PNG companions and clean/paper/stars backgrounds. Existing choices remain available. Share links carry the chosen style; published previews cache that style explicitly.

- **Exhibition:** balanced centered art, growing with sparse shelves and using two rows for five picks.
- **Reading list:** consistent image/text rows with larger readable titles.
- **Collector’s edition:** featured art plus a supporting mosaic and separate title index.

Open `shelf-story-preview.html` through a static server for actual production-rendered alternatives and PNG downloads. Jackets are explicitly synthetic design samples, with no claimed ownership or ratings. Exports are 1080×1920 with 72px side margins and 250px top/bottom Instagram Story reserves. Guide overlays are preview-only and never burned into downloads. Layouts adapt to zero through five actual picks; overview supports every shelf category. Empty shelves never acquire invented picks.

The older `shelf-social-options.html` and comparison sheets are retained as design history. Current examples are the actual `story-*.png` exports in `docs/screenshots/halloween/stories/`.
